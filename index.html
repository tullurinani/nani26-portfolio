A LITTLE CONTEXT

More curious than
finished.

I’m a Trainee Software Engineer drawn to the space where people, applications and permissions meet.

My current focus is Identity Governance and Administration, especially Saviynt EIC. I like understanding the full journey: an identity entering a system, an account being created, an entitlement being assigned, and access being reviewed when something changes.

I learn by doing the unglamorous work too — reading documentation, mapping attributes, testing connections, tracing errors and writing down what I discover. That’s how concepts start to stick.
